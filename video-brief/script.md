# Little Lamb Nannies — Launch Video Script

**Runtime target:** ~4:40 cut (3:52 of narration at ~140 wpm) (rubric: 90–300 s, "around 5 min"; aim 3.5–4.5 min)
**Voice:** David, first person, founder talking to a friend. Short sentences. No hype words.
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps. Real app footage = `video-brief/clips/*.mp4`. AI shots = `AI-*` (prompts come from ChatGPT; see `chatgpt-prompt.md`).

## Structure choice: **Canvas 101 explainer**

The rubric grades five specific questions at 15 points each, so a chaptered explainer — one clear beat per question, each with its own title moment — makes every answer impossible to miss. We borrow one thing from Miro's fast walkthrough (the demo middle moves quickly through real screens) but keep Canvas 101's calm, teach-it-plainly spine because our product's whole pitch is trust, not flash.

## Feature-status rules used in this script

| Status | How it appears |
|---|---|
| **LIVE** (works end to end today) | Real clip, present tense |
| **PARTIAL** (real UI, backend switches on at launch) | Real UI only if shown, narrated as "at launch" / "launching soon" |
| **PLANNED** (spec / backlog only) | **No app UI.** Narration over AI b-roll or the roadmap card |

Pricing is **never** shown or spoken (model not final). Clips were chosen so no platform fee appears on screen.

## Claims check (nothing unbuilt shown as working)

- **LIVE:** application + 4-stage status, admin vetting + verified badges, directory, profile, instant booking, recurring weekly, same-day → job board → first-claim, nanny dashboard/calendar, private reviews, admin override.
- **PARTIAL, narrated as "at launch":** email confirmations, calendar invites, payments (beat 12).
- **PLANNED, roadmap card only:** week view, drag-to-book, one-tap rebooking, mobile app (beat 12).
- **Intro video (beat 7):** the upload step is built into the nanny setup wizard, but the demo data has no videos, so **no video is shown**; the line describes the requirement, not a clip.
- **Process, not software:** in-person interviews, background checks, pastor references happen offline (beats 2, 6, 15).
- **OKRs (beat 13) are a proposal for David and Lucy to confirm**, derived from the analytics targets (1 family : 4 nannies) and the emergency-coverage promise.
